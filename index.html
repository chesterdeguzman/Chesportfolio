About Me

From fearing numbers and presentations to building stories with data.

I earned a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology with a specialization in Business Analytics from Bulacan State University. Choosing analytics was a deliberate challenge: I wanted to move beyond my early fear of numbers, communication, and public speaking.

That decision changed the direction of my career. Through continuous practice, I developed confidence in presenting complex ideas and was recognized as Best Presenter during the 12th In-House Review of Completed Student Research at Bulacan State University in April 2024.

Today, I enjoy transforming raw data into actionable insights using Excel, Python, SQL, Power BI, R, Tableau, SPSS, and related tools. My work combines analytical thinking, data storytelling, visual communication, process automation, and project coordination to help teams make better decisions.
