About Me

From fearing numbers and presentations to building stories with data.

I earned a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology with a specialization in Business Analytics from Bulacan State University. Choosing analytics was a deliberate challenge: I wanted to move beyond my early fear of numbers, communication, and public speaking.
